About AXIA Business Solutions.

AXIA is a pool of enterprise IT specialists serving ports and industrial sites. We design the system, install the hardware, and stay on for support — you deal with one vendor, not five.

What we do

Fixed and wireless networks, business applications, device deployment and system integration — designed, installed and supported by our own engineers.

One vendor, start to finish

Project management, system design, testing, training and support all sit with us. You get a complete solution and a single point of responsibility.

Port-floor experience

Our engineers know the port working environment, so our designs are practical rather than theoretical. That includes rolling out WiFi handheld terminals across live operations.

Port solutions.

P/01

Site survey, before & after

We survey the site before we design anything, and again after handover — to confirm coverage and catch what can be improved.

P/02

Pole installation

P/03

Cabling design

Organised cabling keeps systems running, cuts infrastructure cost, and makes troubleshooting faster when something goes down.

Our partners.

RuggON, founded in 2014, is a rugged computing provider — rugged tablets, vehicle-mount computers, mobile data terminals, monitors and data-capture peripherals built for harsh environments.

Founded in 2006, Point Mobile is one of the fastest-growing enterprise handheld manufacturers — rugged smartphones, mobile payment devices, healthcare terminals, RFID readers and Bluetooth scanners.

Ubiquiti, founded in 2005, manufactures wireless data communication and wired networking products for enterprises and homes.

Bosch was founded in 1886 and is a leading supplier of building technology — heating and air-conditioning systems, video surveillance and fire protection.

Tyco was founded in 1960 and works to advance safety and security — finding smarter ways to protect where people live and work.

Cisco Systems was founded in 1984 and operates worldwide, best known for its computer networking products.
